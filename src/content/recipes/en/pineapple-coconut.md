Recommended glass: a hurricane glass.

## Ingredients

- 30 ml Malibu coconut liqueur
- 10 ml Havana Club 3 Años white rum
- 60 ml Fentel pineapple juice
- 60 ml Coconut Palm coconut drink
- Ice as needed, for shaking
- Ice spears as needed, for serving

## Method

1. Add the coconut liqueur, white rum, pineapple juice, and coconut drink to a shaker.
2. Add ice and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
3. Add ice spears to a glass and strain the drink into it.
