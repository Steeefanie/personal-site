Recommended glass: an Old Fashioned glass.

## Ingredients

- 45 ml Ballantine’s Scotch whisky
- 20 ml Disaronno amaretto
- Square ice cubes as needed, for mixing and serving in the same glass
- 1 cinnamon stick, for smoking

## Method

1. Add square ice cubes to a glass.
2. Add the Scotch whisky and amaretto and stir to combine.
3. Place the cinnamon stick on a heatproof tray. Briefly toast it with a torch until it smokes, then extinguish the flame.
4. Introduce a little cinnamon smoke into the glass to give the drink a smoky aroma.
