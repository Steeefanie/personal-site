Recommended glass: an Old Fashioned glass.

## Ingredients

- 60 ml Ai Wenshan bourbon whiskey
- 30 ml fresh lemon juice
- 10 ml simple syrup
- Ice as needed, for shaking
- Square ice cubes as needed, for serving

## Method

1. Add the whiskey, lemon juice, and simple syrup to a shaker.
2. Add ice and shake hard for about 10–12 seconds.
3. Add square ice cubes to a glass and strain the drink into it.
