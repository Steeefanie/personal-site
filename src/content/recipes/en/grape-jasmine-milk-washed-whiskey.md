Recommended glass: an Old Fashioned glass.

## Ingredients

- 40 ml Jim Beam bourbon whiskey
- 120 ml Bright Dairy whole milk
- 40 ml Wei-Chuan NFC grape juice
- 40 ml Oriental Leaf jasmine tea
- About 10 ml fresh lemon juice, roughly one quarter of a lemon
- 2 teaspoons white sugar, about 8 g
- Square ice cubes as needed, for serving

## Method

1. Combine the whiskey, grape juice, jasmine tea, lemon juice, and sugar. Stir until the sugar dissolves.
2. Pour the milk into a separate container, then slowly pour in the cocktail mixture and stir gently once.
3. Once visible curds have formed, strain through a fine sieve.
4. Slowly pass the liquid through filter paper (a vacuum filtration setup would be even better, if available). If it remains cloudy, return it to the filter and repeat until clear.
5. Add square ice cubes to a glass and pour in the clarified drink.
