Recommended glass: a Collins glass.

## Ingredients

- 15 ml ABSOLUT vodka
- 105 ml original Yakult
- 105 ml Genki Forest white-peach sparkling water
- Ice as needed, for mixing and serving in the same glass

## Method

1. Add ice to a glass.
2. Add the vodka and Yakult and stir to combine.
3. Slowly pour the white-peach sparkling water down the side of the glass.
4. Gently lift the liquid from the bottom of the glass with a bar spoon once or twice to combine.
