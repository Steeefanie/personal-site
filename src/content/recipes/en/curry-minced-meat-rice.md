## Ingredients

- 300 g minced meat;
- ½–1 onion;
- 4–5 garlic cloves;
- 1–2 potatoes;
- about 80 ml uncooked rice by volume;
- whole milk, as needed;
- curry roux blocks, ground black pepper, butter, chilli powder, and light soy sauce, as needed;
- A little cooking oil.

## Method

1. Slice the onion, mince the garlic, and cut the potatoes into chunks. Start cooking the rice at the same time.
2. Put the potatoes in cold water and bring to a boil. Reduce the heat and simmer until just tender but not falling apart, then drain.
3. Heat a little oil in a frying pan. Add the potatoes and fry until golden, then remove and set aside.
4. Add the minced meat to the same pan. Cook over medium heat, breaking it up, until the released liquid has evaporated.
5. Add the garlic and black pepper, stir-fry until evenly mixed, then remove the meat.
6. Add the butter and onion to the same pan. Cook until the onion is soft and golden.
7. Return the meat and add the curry roux. Stir-fry until the roux melts and coats the meat evenly.
8. Add the chilli powder and light soy sauce and stir to combine.
9. Add whole milk as needed and return the potatoes. Simmer over low heat until the meat is cooked through and the sauce is thick.
10. Add the cooked rice, mix thoroughly, and serve.
