Recommended glass: a Collins glass.

## Ingredients

- 40 ml Olmeca Silver tequila
- 60 ml Wei-Chuan grape juice
- 100 ml original Yakult
- Ice as needed, for mixing and serving in the same glass

## Method

1. Add ice to a glass.
2. Add the tequila, grape juice, and Yakult and stir to combine.

Note: A small amount of flocculent sediment will form; if you have a strong stomach, you can ignore it.
