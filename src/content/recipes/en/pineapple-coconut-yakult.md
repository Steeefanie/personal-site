Recommended glass: a Collins glass.

## Ingredients

- 30 ml Malibu coconut liqueur
- 30 ml Fentel pineapple juice
- 30 ml Coconut Palm coconut drink
- 30 ml original Yakult
- Ice as needed, for mixing and serving in the same glass

## Method

1. Add ice to a glass.
2. Add the coconut liqueur, pineapple juice, coconut drink, and Yakult and stir to combine.
