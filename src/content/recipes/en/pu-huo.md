Recommended glass: a Collins glass.

## Ingredients

- 15 ml Soviet Red vodka
- 15 ml Hayman’s gin
- 30 ml NFC grape juice
- 140 ml lemon- or grapefruit-flavored vitamin C drink
- Ice as needed, for shaking
- Fresh ice as needed, for serving

## Method

1. Add the vodka, gin, and grape juice to a shaker.
2. Add ice and shake for about 10 seconds.
3. Add fresh ice to a glass and pour in the Shui Rong C.
4. Strain the shaken mixture, then slowly pour it over the back of a bar spoon into the glass. Do not stir afterward.
