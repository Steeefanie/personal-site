Recommended glass: a 30 ml shot glass.

## Ingredients

- Olmeca silver tequila, as needed
- A little Tabasco pepper sauce

## Method

1. Pour the silver tequila into a glass, leaving a little room at the top.
2. Slowly drip the Tabasco down the inside of the glass. Let it settle at the bottom without stirring.

Tip: Best taken in one gulp; it works well for keeping the cold at bay.
